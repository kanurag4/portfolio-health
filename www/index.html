Data & methodology notes
  • Data source: Yahoo Finance via a proxy — may be delayed or incomplete for some tickers.
  • ETF sectors: sourced from Yahoo's sectorWeightings module, which covers the full fund.
  • ETF regions: sourced from Yahoo's country data where available. When unavailable, estimated from the top-10 holding ticker suffixes only (.AX = Australia, no suffix = United States, .L = UK, etc.). Weight outside the top 10 appears as Unclassified.
  • Stock concentration: ETF look-through covers top-10 holdings only. Sub-holdings below that threshold are excluded from concentration flags.
  • Mixed $ / % inputs are normalised proportionally — see the "Weights normalised" note if shown.
